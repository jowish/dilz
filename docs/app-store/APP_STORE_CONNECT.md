## Promotional copy

## Description

Dilz helps people discover and share worthwhile deals in Israel.

Browse community finds, compare supermarket promotions, vote on useful deals, save favorites and create alerts for the products and places that matter to you. Use the map to explore nearby offers, scan a barcode to compare products, and share verified deals with the community.

Dilz supports English and Hebrew.

## Privacy labels to verify in App Store Connect

Data linked to the user and used for app functionality:

- Contact Info: email address and name.
- User Content: photos/videos, posts and comments.
- Identifiers: account identifier and native push token once APNs is enabled.
- Purchases: not collected by Dilz.
- Financial information: not collected.
- Tracking: no.

Data not linked to the user:

- Precise location is processed only after the user selects My location, to resolve a nearby city. Dilz should not persist raw coordinates.
