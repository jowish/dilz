# App Review notes draft

Dilz is a community shopping utility for Israel. The app includes user-generated deal posts and comments.

## Review account

- Email: `[CREATE A DEDICATED REVIEW ACCOUNT]`
- Password: `[SET A REVIEW PASSWORD]`

The account must already be email-confirmed and must not have admin access.

## Features to review

1. The initial Dilz feed shows active community deals.
2. The central Post action creates a deal and supports up to three photos.
3. The three-dot menu on deals and comments provides Report and Block user controls.
4. Profile > Account settings contains language, feed preference, Privacy Policy, Terms, Support and Delete account.
5. Delete account permanently removes the account and private data and anonymizes public contributions.
6. The city selector requests location only after My location is selected.
7. The camera is requested only when you choose to add a photo while posting or editing a deal.

## Moderation

Submitted reports appear in the protected admin dashboard. Administrators can review, action or dismiss reports, delete deals/comments and ban abusive users. Reports must be monitored during review and after release.

## Business model

Dilz does not sell digital goods and has no in-app purchases in version 1.0. Merchant links open external physical-goods offers.
